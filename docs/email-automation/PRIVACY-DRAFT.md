# Privacy policy: wording needed before the nurture emails go live

Read from https://www.kygo.app/privacy-policy on 2026-10-26 (Last Updated: October 2, 2026). Not changed.

## Finding

The policy does not cover this. It never mentions website email sign-ups, email click tracking or a Google Sheet. Section 4.1 lists Google Workspace for "transactional email such as password resets and replies to your support requests" only. Section 7.4 says: "We do not send marketing email. ... If we ever introduce marketing communications, they will be opt-in and this section will be updated first." The report emails are marketing email, so the policy has to change before the first one sends. (Sign-up is already opt-in: the report form says "One email with the PDF, then four short follow-ups. Unsubscribe anytime.")

## Exact text to add (4 places)

**Section 2.6 (The kygo.app Website), new bullet after "If you contact us...":**

> If you sign up on kygo.app for a report or for email updates, your email address, the page you signed up on, and the date. We use them to send what you asked for. For the State of Wearable Accuracy report that is the report email and four follow-up emails over the next two weeks. We record when each email was sent, which links in it you click, and whether an email bounced or you unsubscribed. Links in these emails carry a random code, never your email address. We do not track whether you open our emails.

**Section 4.1, replace the Google Workspace entry with:**

> Google Workspace (email and records): Delivery of transactional email such as password resets and replies to your support requests, and of the emails you sign up for on kygo.app. Our list of email sign-ups, with send dates, link clicks, unsubscribes and bounces, is kept in a Google Sheet in our own Google Workspace account and processed there by Google Apps Script, under Google's Workspace data processing terms. It does not contain any health, nutrition or wearable data and is not connected to your Kygo account.

**Section 6 (retention), new bullet after "Website analytics and advertising measurement":**

> Email sign-ups: we keep your email address and its send, click and unsubscribe records while you are subscribed. After you unsubscribe, or 12 months after our last email to you, we delete the click records and keep only your address and the unsubscribe date, so we never email you again by mistake. Email us to have that removed too.

**Section 7.4 (Contact Preferences), replace the whole paragraph with:**

> We send marketing email only to people who ask for it on kygo.app, such as the State of Wearable Accuracy report and its four follow-up emails. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link, supports one-click unsubscribe in your mail app, and lets you reply with the word unsubscribe. Unsubscribing stops them straight away. The other email you receive from us is limited to essential service messages such as password resets, security notices, replies to your support requests, and notices about changes to these policies or your subscription. Reminders and other push notifications are controlled by you under Settings in the app and in your device settings.

**Optional, section 13 (European Privacy Rights, the "Our legal bases are" sentence): add to the consent list:**

> ... and your consent for the emails you sign up for on kygo.app, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.

Then update "Last Updated" at the top and bottom.
