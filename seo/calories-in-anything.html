Kygo Food Scanner — Free AI-powered tool by Kygo Health. Snap a photo of any meal to get instant calories, macros, health score (0–100), and detailed nutrition insights including protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, and sodium. Powered by AI image recognition trained on over 5 million foods from USDA and international nutrition databases. Identifies individual ingredients, portion sizes, and cooking methods from a single photo. No signup or download required — works instantly in your browser.

Our AI identifies ingredients and estimates portion sizes using visual analysis. Results are reliable for everyday tracking and meal awareness. For clinical dietary planning, we recommend pairing this with a registered dietitian. Accuracy improves with well-lit, overhead photos of plated meals.

Every scan returns calories, six macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium), a 1–10 health score, key vitamins and minerals with % daily value, dietary tags like High Protein or Heart Healthy, and actionable health insights specific to that food.

JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC (iPhone photos). You can upload from your camera roll, take a photo directly, or drag and drop. Images are compressed client-side before analysis—your original photo never leaves your device.

This scanner gives you instant one-off nutrition checks. The Kygo app takes it further—log meals daily and connect your wearable (Oura, Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Whoop, or Health Connect) to discover how specific foods affect your sleep, HRV, energy, and recovery over time.

You get 20 free scans per day with no sign-up required. The Kygo app includes unlimited AI-powered food logging along with wearable correlations and a free forever plan.

The AI will detect non-food items and let you know. You'll see a notice that the image doesn't appear to be food, along with a prompt to try again with a meal photo.
