Quick answers

Is Kygo really free?

Yes, there's a free tier that's free forever. It includes voice, barcode, and text logging, all wearable connections, health trends, and macro/micronutrient tracking, plus 5 AI photo scans a month.

Pro adds food-body insights, supplement tracking, unlimited photo logging, a daily factor spotlight, and nutrition write-back to Apple Health & Health Connect. Upgrade anytime.

How is Kygo different from MyFitnessPal?

MyFitnessPal tracks calories for weight loss. Kygo shows you how food affects your sleep, HRV, energy, and recovery.

We correlate your nutrition with wearable data to find patterns unique to YOUR body.

Which wearables do you support?

Oura Ring, Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Polar, and Health Connect. Connect one or multiple.
